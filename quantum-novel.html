The Book

A startup wager against hard quantum physics

The Quantum Minima: Beyond Classical is a hard science-fiction techno-thriller set in Bengaluru: a founder trio running a quantum-hardware build out of a Peenya workshop, sourcing what they can't afford on S.P. Road, and chasing the physics that gets taken seriously in IISc's clean rooms. It runs on the same operating logic that shapes real Indian startups: do more with less, decide fast, own what you decided. The story starts pulling at that logic the moment a piece of hardware starts making calls nobody coded into it.

Bengaluru startup corridors tight-budget founder realism AI decision-ownership hard-SF quantum computing techno-thriller

"The defect wasn't a flaw. It was the first thing the diamond ever decided on its own."

Where the defect leads, what the founders choose, and what "deciding" turns out to mean here stay on the page. That's what the chapters are for. What's public is the shape: quantum computing that doesn't cut corners on the physics, wrapped around the question of who's really in control when the machine has an opinion.
