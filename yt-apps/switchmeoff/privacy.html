SwitchMeOff Publisher: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21.

Who uses this app

Only the channel owner, signing in with their own Google account. There are no other users.

Data accessed

Through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API the app reads the owner's channel details, video list, comments on the owner's videos, and channel statistics (views, watch time, subscribers). It writes only the owner's own video uploads and metadata.

Storage and use

The authorisation token is stored on the owner's own computers and is used only to upload and read for this channel. Statistics are used only by the owner to plan their own content. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models.

Google API Services

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

This tool is used by an adult (the channel owner). It is not directed to children and does not collect any information from children or from viewers. The channel's videos are made for children and are published under YouTube's own Made for Kids controls; any viewer data stays inside YouTube.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete stored tokens from their computers.
