SwitchMeOff Publisher

SwitchMeOff Publisher is a private publishing tool run by its owner for the SwitchMeOff YouTube channel (calm screen-free short stories for children). It uploads the owner's own videos, sets their titles, descriptions and schedule, and reads the channel's own view and subscriber statistics so the owner can see how videos perform.

It is not offered to the public. Nobody else signs in to it; only the channel owner authorises it, with their own Google account.

What it asks Google for

  • youtube.upload: upload the owner's videos to their own channel.
  • youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly: read the channel's own statistics and video list.
  • youtube.force-ssl: read and answer comments on the owner's own videos.
